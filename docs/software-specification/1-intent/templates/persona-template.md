## Role

Describe the persona's relevant role in the workflow.

## Desired Outcomes

- State the outcomes this person needs to achieve.
- Keep outcomes observable and relevant to product decisions.

## Triggers and Behaviors

- Describe what starts the interaction.
- Describe the steps, decisions, and information needs involved.
- Describe behaviors that indicate confusion, progress, or abandonment.

## Constraints

- Record time, knowledge, access, policy, platform, or organizational constraints.
- Include demographic details only when they affect behavior or constraints.

## Pain Points

- Describe observed or reported obstacles.
- Link pain points to evidence where possible.

## Success Criteria

- Describe how this persona recognizes successful completion.
- Use criteria that can inform requirements or acceptance tests.

## Evidence and Confidence

- Sources: List research, analytics, support, interviews, or workflow observations.
- Confidence: low, medium, or high.
- Review date: YYYY-MM-DD.

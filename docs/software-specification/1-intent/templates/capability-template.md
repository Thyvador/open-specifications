## Purpose and Outcome

Describe the meaningful user or business outcome this capability exists to create.

## Supported Personas

Link primary, secondary, indirect, and negatively affected personas.

## Scope

Describe the product behavior and outcomes included in this capability.

## Non-Goals

Describe related capabilities, users, or outcomes explicitly excluded.

## Related Features

Link several related feature documents. Detailed behavior belongs in those feature documents.

- [Feature name](/specs/features/<feature>/feature.md)
- [Another feature](/specs/features/<another-feature>/feature.md)

## Expected Behavior

Describe what the product should enable, prevent, or communicate at capability level. Do not specify UI components, architecture, technology, APIs, or implementation details.

## Constraints and Dependencies

Record product, legal, security, accessibility, performance, reliability, compatibility, and operational constraints. Link dependencies without prescribing their implementation.

## Success Metrics

Link PRD metrics and describe how capability progress contributes to them.
